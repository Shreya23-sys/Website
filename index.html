Core Pillars

Preserving Heritage Through Creative Expression

Kala Laya Nepal bridges ancient Himalayan artistry with fearless modern storytelling, empowering local communities through theater, dance, and craft.

Stage Drama

Community Theatre

From social satire plays to classic Nepali folk dramas, we bring theatre outside institutional boundaries and directly into town squares and schools.

Discover our story →
Sacred Rituals

Masked Dance & Rituals

Honoring the wrathful compassion of the Lakhey and Bhairav traditions with authentic wooden masks, rhythmic footwork, and sacred ceremony.

View dance gallery →
Workshops

Fine Arts & Paubha

Hands-on studios teaching the grinding of mineral pigments, canvas stretching, and the delicate iconography of Paubha and vibrant Mithila art.

Folk Ensemble

Living Folk Music

The resonant heart of Nepali narrative: preserving rare Gandharva Sarangi melodies, Basuri flute lines, and resonant Madal rhythms.
