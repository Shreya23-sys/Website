Our Roots & Mission

A Living Theatre of the Himalayas

How a group of determined theatre makers in Itahari forged a nationwide movement for Nepali performing and sacred arts.

12+ Years Active in Grassroots Arts
Genesis

Art Born from Community Passion

Kala Laya (कलालय) was born in Itahari, Sunsari, driven by the vision of theatre director and cultural activist Sonu Jayanti and a close-knit ensemble of passionate artists.

Unlike institutional theatres with dedicated auditoriums in the capital city, Kalalaya grew in the open air, school courtyards, and rented community spaces. What could have been an insurmountable constraint became our defining philosophy: theatre must belong directly to the people.

We invite students, local housewives, shopkeepers, and aspiring creators into the rehearsal room, turning collective anxieties and joys into compelling stage productions that speak with authenticity.

Preservation

Beyond Modern Stage: Reviving Sacred Crafts

At Kala Laya Nepal, our stage is inseparably bound to the sacred visual arts of the Himalayas. We don't just rehearse lines; we study the sacred geometry of wooden masks, the devotional brushwork of Paubha and Mithila painting, and the polyrhythms of Newari drums and Gandharva folk instruments.

Every wooden mask worn in our ritual dramas is carved by master artisans honoring centuries-old traditions. Through our educational workshops, we train youth so that these ancient skills never fade from Nepal's living memory.

Milestones

The Kala Laya Journey

2014

First Rehearsals in Itahari

Sonu Jayanti convenes youth and students for grassroots theatre workshops in Sunsari, establishing the collective foundation of Kalalaya.

2017

Staging of Classic 'Refund'

Kalalaya produces its critically acclaimed adaptation of Karinthy's 'Refund', touring schools and community stages across Eastern Nepal.

2021

Sacred Mask & Fine Arts Initiative

Launch of cross-disciplinary workshops combining traditional Bhairav/Lakhey masks, Paubha painting, and live acoustic music ensembles.

2023

Nepal International Theatre Festival (NITFest)

Featured in national and international festivals, showcasing regional Nepali theatre to global audiences and critics.

2026

Global Community & Worldwide Access

Launching our digital hub connecting international patrons, students, and diaspora with living Nepali theatrical and artistic heritage.
